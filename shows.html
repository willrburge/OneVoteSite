About the Musical

One Vote! The Musical is a new original work by Larry Groce and Todd Burge, directed by Jack Colclough*, and presented by The Northwest Territory Museum Society. The production brings a powerful piece of Marietta and Northwest Territory history to the stage in America’s 250th year.

Made possible in part through support from The Ohio Arts Council, America 250-Ohio, Marietta College, The Center for Appalachian Innovation, and private donors.

Executive Producer: The Northwest Territory Museum Society

*Jack Colclough - Director of the School of Theatre & Dance, Marshall University
